Irvine Drywall FAQs

What drywall services do you offer?

We provide drywall installation, repair, replacement, finishing, taping, mudding, sanding, and texture work for residential properties.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call (949) 592-1961 or submit the estimate form to tell us about your project.

Do you handle small drywall repairs?

Yes. We can help with small holes and damaged sections as well as larger drywall projects.

Do you work on both walls and ceilings?

Yes. Drywall installation and repair are available for both walls and ceilings.

Can you repair drywall after plumbing or electrical work?

Yes. We can close and finish openings made to access plumbing, wiring, or other systems.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Irvine and nearby Orange County communities including Tustin, Costa Mesa, Newport Beach, and Lake Forest.

How do I request an estimate?

Call (949) 592-1961, email contact@drywallirvine.com, or complete our online estimate form.
